# LONGGAME-DOCTRINE.md — authoring law for the call-form bank
2026-07-27. Extends PUZZLE-DOCTRINE.md (whose per-node gates all still apply) with the laws of the Long Game form and the dialogue craft that kills sterility. Ratified direction: the Long Game is the body; refutation continuations are its fabric; mark-the-moment and hold-or-speak are node types inside it; type-first (production bridge, DetectorLocal) is a mode above any node. Aesthetic is LOCKED: the app's existing chess.com-dark skin (#262421 / #312E2B / #81B64C, SF rounded-heavy, Verdict vocabulary) — content work never touches it.

## I · Form laws
1. **Spine, not tree.** A call is 4–6 fixed beats. State (a scalar deal-temperature + boolean flags) varies each beat's buyer OPENING line (≤3 variants) — never the beat structure. Endings branch fully (3–4 authored). Combinatorial authoring is forbidden; consequence comes from:
2. **Scars.** One-line callbacks to earlier picks ("This is slide four, isn't it."). Cheap, devastating, mandatory: ≥2 per call.
3. **Node types rotate.** pick-the-line · mark-the-moment (buyer monologue; tap where you'd come in) · hold-or-speak (a live sidebar between buyers; interject or stay out). No call is all-pick. Silence/holding must be the key somewhere in the bank and NOT in a fixed ratio (no meta-tell).
4. **Mid-call feedback is diegetic ONLY.** The room reacts (she writes it down / the pen stops / he checks his phone); no verdicts, glyphs, meters, or deltas until the Read-Back. A verdict shown mid-call is a leak that teaches guess-and-check.
5. **The Read-Back is the lesson.** Post-call: per-node verdict (app Verdict vocab, exact glyphs/colors), what-the-room-was-telling-you, master line, the UNPLAYED branches revealed, deal-trajectory sparkline (the eval graph), and the director's layer — every buyer turn re-shown with its intent unmasked. First reading is the game; second reading is the lesson.
6. **The Fork is conditional and scarce.** `isFork` fires only on the flagged best line played on a qualifying path (the concession that wins two fronts must have actually been set up). Never inflated; most calls have none.

## II · Dialogue laws (the sterility killers)
7. **Write intent first, surface second.** Every buyer turn is authored twice: the director's note (what they mean / want / hide — this drives grading) and the spoken line (which conceals most of it). If surface ≈ intent, the turn is a caption, not dialogue — rewrite.
8. **Every load-bearing signal leaks twice, differently.** One leak = unfair astrology; announcement = sterile. Two independent leaks (a verbal aside + a behavior; a hedge + a silence) make the read checkable without making it loud.
9. **Fingerprints.** Each persona has a voice sheet: lexicon, rhythm, deflection style, pressure behavior, and one PERSONAL tell (e.g. Priya: clipped fragments = engaged, fluent politeness = leaving; Tom: "yeah, no—" = no; goes quiet when he disagrees). Fingerprints are consistent across the whole bank — they are learnable content, and the director's layer teaches them.
10. **Dirtying pass.** After the clean draft: interruptions, restarts, non-answers, info arriving in subordinate clauses mid-complaint, at least one question answered with a different answer. Real speech has noise; noise is what reads as human.
11. **The curriculum-shape ban.** If a line could appear in a sales book as an example — polished discovery question, framework phrasing, aphorism — it fails. This applies to OPTIONS and VERDICT-WHY copy too: options are what a person says under pressure; why-copy is a coach who was listening, not a fortune cookie.
12. **Friction is mandatory.** Every character has a reason not to fully cooperate with the conversation (time, boredom, turf, history). A cooperative room is a sterile room.
13. **Numbers don't round; the world recurs.** 41 spokes, not 40. Companies, competitors, and people recur across the bank as texture (never as rating-relevant continuity). It's a territory, not a question bank.

## III · Epistemic honesty of noise
14. **True tells are the evidenced ones** (re-anchored Atlas + corpus): unbounded dependencies, single-threading, indecision language, next-step vagueness, approval-without-date. These are what marking/reading rewards.
15. **Folk tells are bait.** Mirroring theater, filler-word psychology, body-language astrology appear in scenes and PUNISH the player who chases them. The game teaches which half of sales folklore is real. Every red herring must be a documented folk tell or a genuine-but-irrelevant fact — never arbitrary.
16. **Each node's key cites its evidence** in the call's design doc (Atlas tier / corpus unit / JOLT). No node ships keyed to fame or vibes.

## IV · Gates (run before Marion ever sees a call)
17. **Recoverability band.** N≥3 fresh-context blind readers annotate perceived intent per buyer turn (no notes visible). Load-bearing turns must land in the band: recovered by SOME but not ALL readers (target ~40–85%). ~100% = on-the-nose; ~0% = unfair. Off-band → rewrite the leaks, not the options.
18. **Cover-the-options.** An options-only agent (sees choices, not the scene) must not beat chance materially at picking keys. PUZZLE-DOCTRINE's length/tag balance per node.
19. **Ban-list lint** (mechanical): lab-speak (operator/recipient/prospect-facing jargon), curriculum shapes, rounded numbers in buyer facts.
20. **Verdict agreement.** Same eval bands as `Verdict.from` (Models/Verdict.swift) so HTML prototypes and the app never tell different stories.
21. **Human gates.** Marion's read (taste, final) · key-correctness: adjudication panel when it exists, else the call ships PROVISIONAL (unrated → self-calibrating item ratings; promotion needs panel sign-off or convergent data).

## V · Per-call pipeline
design doc (evidence per node, state arc, fork placement or explicitly none, difficulty target) → spine skeleton (beats, node types, variants, scars, endings) → intent notes → surface dialogue (fingerprints) → dirtying pass → gates 17–20 → Marion → provisional pool.
Throughput honesty: a call is 1,500–2,500 authored words; 2–3 calls per session at bar. Launch tier 25–30 calls (≈100–140 rated nodes). The old 127 items demote to unrated Fundamentals; salvage keys by re-dramatization only.
