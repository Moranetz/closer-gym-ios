Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-05-13. Applies to the Frame & Fork iOS app.

Plain English summary

Frame & Fork does not collect personal data. There is no account, no server-side database, no tracking, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, and no advertising.

The Free tier (Daily Drill, Puzzles, Lessons, Master Games, Watch) is fully offline. Nothing leaves your device.

The Pro tier (bot ladder) requires you to provide your own Anthropic API key. That key is sent directly from your device to Anthropic to generate conversation responses. Frame & Fork does not receive, log, or store your conversations.

What is stored on your device

  • Puzzle solves and ratings. Stored locally via UserDefaults under the key framefork:puzzles:v0.1. Includes which puzzles you solved, your puzzle Glicko-2 rating, your current and longest streaks.
  • Pro tier API key (optional). If you enable the Pro tier, your Anthropic API key is stored in the iOS Keychain. Never transmitted to any Frame & Fork server (there is no server). Used only to call Anthropic's API from your device.
  • Sparring session transcripts (Pro tier). If you play a Pro bot game, the transcript is stored locally for the post-game review. Never uploaded.

What is NOT collected

  • Name, email, phone number, or any identifier
  • Device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV, advertising IDs)
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, camera
  • Usage analytics or crash reports beyond what Apple provides at the system level
  • Health or financial data

Third-party services

The Free tier uses no third-party services. The Pro tier sends conversation text directly to Anthropic using the API key you supply. Anthropic's privacy policy governs what they do with that data; Frame & Fork is not a party to that processing.

Children

Frame & Fork is intended for users 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Data deletion

Open Settings inside the app and tap Clear all data. This deletes your puzzle solves, ratings, streaks, Pro tier API key, and any sparring session transcripts from the device. There is no cloud copy because nothing is uploaded.

Changes to this policy

Material changes will be reflected in the effective date at the top of this page and announced in the app release notes.
